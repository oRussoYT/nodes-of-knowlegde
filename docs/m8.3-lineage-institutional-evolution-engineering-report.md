## 2. Exact scope implemented

C2 lineage, two lenses: institutional succession/formation descent (kinds
`succession` + `merger`) inline on `/institutions/[organizationId]`; documented
mentorship descent (kind `mentorship`) inline on `/people/[personId]`. Each a
bounded (depth 16), cycle-safe transitive chain of same-kind directional edges,
upstream (antecedents/mentors) and downstream (successors/students).

## 3. Historical question answered

"What documented chain of same-kind directional assertions does this institution's
succession / this person's mentorship sit within — who precedes and who follows?"

## 4. Deterministic rule

Recursive traversal of explicit directional edges: upstream follows edges whose
`target` is the current node; downstream follows edges whose `source` is the current
node. Depth-capped at 16; cycle-safe via a visited `path` array; each canonical edge
de-duplicated to its minimum depth (`distinct on (rel_id)`); neutral ordering (depth,
endpoint name, id). Order is what the assertions carry (source→target); no gap is ever
filled.

## 5. Canonical source assertions

`organization_relationships` (kind ∈ {succession, merger}) + `organizations` +
`organization_relationship_kinds`; `relationships` (kind = mentorship) + `people` +
`relationship_kinds`. No new table, no write.

## 7. Database / security audit

Two additive functions; no table/column/RLS/write. `SECURITY DEFINER`,
`search_path` pinned, `auth.uid()` required in-body, revoked from `PUBLIC`, granted to
`authenticated`. Merged people excluded from mentorship chains; merged/nonexistent
focal → null (person); nonexistent institution → null. Bounded (depth cap 16) and
cycle-safe (visited-path guard) — a finite, inspectable configuration (Spec §6.6).
Only the descent kinds are followed; governance kinds are excluded by the `kind in
(...)` / `kind = 'mentorship'` predicates. Every referenced column matches the M6.4/
M7 schema audited during scope determination.

## 8. Projection / parser contract audit

Every projected node is emitted in the canonical `ProjectedNode` shape (`type`, `id`,
`label`, `secondary_label`, `href`, `verification_status`); pgTAP locks it (focal
node, and step endpoints, assert `type` and `label`). The parser (`parse-lineage.ts`)
reuses the shared `parseNode`/`parseTemporal` primitives, is fail-closed (drops a step
with a missing/mistyped endpoint, missing source, bad direction, non-numeric depth, or
missing source-role; requires `upstream`/`downstream` arrays), and Vitest confirms the
SQL→parser shape.

## 9. Temporal / provenance audit

Each step shows its own stored dates (shared temporal kernel); nothing is inferred or
back-filled. Each step carries its full provenance envelope and its canonical
`source` row; a juxtaposition of two records preserves each side's provenance/temporal
independently (each endpoint is its own node, each step its own row). A gap is shown
as the chain ending — never a filled link.

## 10. Production integration

Inline on both canonical pages inside the shared `ReadingSpine`, after the existing
engines (institution: after co-presence; person: after cohorts). No new route,
navigation, dashboard, metric, or graph. Heading outline: section h2 → direction-group
h3 → step list items; no skipped level; both endpoints of every step are doorways.

**Reader-orientation refinement (post-implementation reading-experience review).**
A historian-reading review asked whether the reader immediately sees where the
current subject sits in the chain. The direction groups alone left the focal subject
implicit (named only inside step sentences), so a minimal, presentation-only anchor
was added: a non-interactive "you are reading" card naming the focal node, placed
between the antecedents/mentors and the successors/students. It uses the focal node
already in the document; no SQL, traversal, data, capability, route, metric, or
terminology change. Copy gains one neutral `subjectLabel` per lens; the Playwright
specs assert the anchor; pure-logic remains 51/51.

## 13. Defects found and fixed

Two, caught in static self-review before the Mac run: (a) a **diamond in the relation
graph** would emit the same canonical edge twice under `UNION ALL` — fixed with
`distinct on (rel_id)` (min-depth) in all four projections, keeping output
deterministic and each edge decomposed once; (b) the **stale-staging trap** — the
person page's committed M8.1 integration was verified present on the device (git
`cb6e540`) after a staged snapshot appeared to lack it, so no phantom "regression" was
introduced. No production logic defect otherwise.

## 14. Constitutional audit (M8 §9.1)

Read-only (functions only); no inference (only explicit directional edges of the named
kinds are followed — pgTAP: governance/advising/merged excluded, no lineage from names/
chronology/shared personnel); no metric/ranking/centrality (neutral order; chains never
ranked by length; depth shown for honesty, not rank); deterministic + bounded (depth
cap, cycle-safe, `distinct on`; pgTAP determinism + cycle-termination); decomposable
(every step → its canonical row; both endpoints reachable); order not causation
("followed what", never "followed from what" — copy tested); temporally honest (own
dates, no fill); fail-closed security; honest incompleteness (limits + empty state);
no new destination (inline; nav unchanged); no interpretation (structural "descent"/
"lineage", never school/tradition/influence/transmission/evolution — copy unit-tested
to exclude them).

## 15. Limitations

Depth cap 16 (a chain longer than 16 hops upstream/downstream is truncated at the cap —
deterministic and bounded per Spec §6.6; noted, not silent). Representative min-depth
per edge (a diamond shows each edge once at its shortest distance). Mentorship chain is
`mentorship`-only (advising and other directional kinds are separate homogeneous
lenses, deferred). Exhibition inspection deferred.
