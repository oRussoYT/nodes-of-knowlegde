# M8.5 — Revelation Engine: documented recurrence (C4)

Status: implemented (pending local Mac validation). See
`docs/decisions/0018-revelation-engine.md` (amended for M8.5), the M8 Definitive
Specification (§3.5 recurrence counting, §4 count-not-metric), and the M8
Engineering Blueprint.

## What this is

M8.1/M8.2 revealed **co-presence**, M8.3 **lineage**, M8.4 **continuity &
rupture**. M8.5 reveals a fifth pattern family: **documented recurrence** — that
the historical record itself documents the **same phenomenon occurring multiple
independent times** for one focal entity. Recurrence is **not similarity**:
nothing is compared, clustered, embedded, mined, or inferred. Only distinct
**explicit** assertions of the same structural kind generate a recurrence, and
only when there are at least **two** of them. Two lenses, each read **inline**:

- **`reveal_person_recurrence(uuid)`** — on `/people/[personId]`, after the
  co-presence and lineage engines: the same participation **role** at the same
  institution (participations grouped by organization + capacity), documented
  **events** of the same kind (`person_events → events` by `event_kind`), and
  documented **contributions** of the same kind (`person_contributions →
  contributions` by `contribution_kind`).
- **`reveal_organization_recurrence(uuid)`** — on `/institutions/[organizationId]`,
  after the continuity engine: documented **events** of the same kind and
  documented **contributions** of the same kind.

## The reader's question

"What has happened **repeatedly**?" — not "what seems similar?" The reader sees
what recurred, how many documented occurrences exist, their chronological order,
and where documentation is missing.

## Scope decision (ratified, reported not silent)

Spec §3.5 names C4's "recurrence counting" **dyadically** (the count of distinct
contexts in which the same *pair* co-appears). The M8.5 mission defines
recurrence as **single-entity repetition** ("the same phenomenon occurred
multiple independent times"). The product owner ratified the **single-entity**
reading for this milestone (and the person + institution surfaces); the dyadic
co-appearance count remains a deferred sibling. Both obey the identical
constitutional discipline: a count of distinct explicit assertions, never a
metric.

**Institution scope clarification.** The institution lens **excludes
participations**. Per-capacity coverage over time at an institution is M8.4's
continuity lens; reading it again as a recurrence count would be a duplicative
reading of the same rows. The institution recurrence therefore composes only over
the institution's own repeated **events** and **contributions**. (Reversible if
participation-recurrence is later wanted.)

## The deterministic rule

For a focal entity, group its **own** explicit assertions by a structural
recurrence key and reveal each group that holds **≥ 2** distinct occurrences:

| category | source | recurrence key | occurrence node |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `role` | `participations` | organization + capacity | — (institution is the group anchor) |
| `event` | `person_events`/`organization_events` → `events` | `event_kind` | event node (title; no page) |
| `contribution` | `person_contributions`/`organization_contributions` → `contributions` | `contribution_kind` | contribution node (doorway to its page) |

Each group carries a plain **count** (= number of occurrences), the occurrences
in **time order** (undated last), and — for a role — the institution as a
`ProjectedNode` anchor. Groups are ordered **neutrally** (category, then label,
then a stable key) — **never by count**. The count is literally the number of
distinct explicit assertions of that kind, and is presented as "Documented N
times as …"; it is never a tie-strength, weight, rank, or a measure of
importance, activity, or standing (Spec §4).

## Honest incompleteness

A single documented occurrence is **not** recurrence (no group). An **undated**
occurrence still counts (the record documents that it happened; only its date is
unknown) and is shown last, marked unknown — unknown periods remain unknown.
Absence of recurrence is a dignified honest absence, never a claim that nothing
recurred. Two records of the same real occurrence would each be counted (the
count is of distinct records) — stated in the limits.

## Feature module (reuse)

Reuses the `parse-shared` primitives, `ProjectedNode`, the temporal/provenance
kernels, `RevealedPeriod`, `ProvenanceAffordance`, and the `revelationCopy`
module. To avoid duplication, the shared node parser was **generalized once**: a
new `parseAnyNode` accepts all four `NetworkNodeType`s (a recurrence occurrence
may be a contribution or event node), and the existing `parseNode` now delegates
to it and restricts to person/organization — so M8.1–M8.4 keep their exact prior
contract. Adds `parse-recurrence.ts`, the `RecurrenceGroup`/`RecurrenceOccurrence`
type family, `read`/`derive`/`copy` lenses, and three components
(`PersonRecurrence`, `OrganizationRecurrence`, shared `RecurrenceGroupList`).

## Terminology

Structural terms only: *documented recurrence / occurrence / count / of the same
kind*. Copy never says frequent, prolific, prominent, notable, significant,
influential, important, popular, or top; it never ranks; and it states plainly
that a count is a count of records, not a measure of activity or standing. Copy
is unit-tested to exclude that vocabulary.

## Future compatibility with M9

M9 (interpretation) is where meaning belongs. M8.5 hands M9 a clean,
decomposable substrate: each recurrence is a set of canonical assertion ids with
their temporal and provenance, and no verdict of its own. M9 may interpret *why*
something recurred, or whether a recurrence is significant; M8.5 only reveals
*that* the record documents it, and how many times — leaving every judgement to
the reader and to M9.

order proven by a count-2 group preceding a count-3 group of the same category by
label; undated occurrences counting and sorting last; the ProjectedNode contract
and canonical-source decomposition incl. contribution/event node types;
institution participations excluded; honest empty/nonexistent; determinism;
fail-closed auth and merged exclusion). Vitest (parse/derive/copy: fail-closed
dropping, the ≥ 2 rule, count-equals-occurrences, banned vocabulary). Playwright
(`revelation-recurrence.spec.ts`: person + institution surfaces inline, counted
recurrence, doorways, honest limits and absence, a11y/375px/dark/console).
