# M8.3 — Revelation Engine: lineage & institutional evolution (C2)

Status: implemented (pending local Mac validation). See
`docs/decisions/0018-revelation-engine.md` (amended for M8.3), the M8 Definitive
Specification (§3.4 sequence composition, §7.4), and the M8 Engineering Blueprint.

## What this is

M8.1 and M8.2 revealed **co-presence** (a shared term + temporal overlap). M8.3
reveals a different pattern family: the documented **lineage** — a bounded chain
of **same-kind directional** relationship Assertions read across generations
(Spec §1: "lineage means a chain of same-kind relationship assertions, not a
tradition"). Two lenses, each read **inline** on its canonical page:

- **`reveal_organization_lineage(uuid)`** — the institution's documented
  succession/formation descent (kinds `succession` + `merger`), on
  `/institutions/[organizationId]`, deepening M7's one-hop institutional lineage
  to the full transitive chain.
- **`reveal_person_mentorship_lineage(uuid)`** — the documented mentorship descent
  (kind `mentorship`, Mentor→Student), on `/people/[personId]`.

Each traverses the explicit directional edges upstream (antecedents / mentors) and
downstream (successors / students), bounded (depth-capped at 16), cycle-safe
(visited-path guard), each step decomposable to its exact canonical relationship
row, both endpoints projected as `ProjectedNode` doorways. It records **what
followed what, never what followed from what** (Spec §3.4): no transmission,
influence, school, tradition, or causation.

## Scope determination

M8.3 = C2 only. **Comparison C5 is enabled by M8.3's lineage juxtaposition but not
assigned to it** (the blueprint places C5 in M8.2 — deferred — and assigns only C2
to M8.3); C5 remains deferred, to be taken up as a small same-pattern addition when
you choose, not built speculatively here.

**Scope clarification (reported, not silently resolved).** The blueprint's C2 body
illustrates the institutional descent as "succession/merger/**administration**."
`administration` (Administering→Administered), `parent_body`, and `hosting` are
**governance/structural** relations, not temporal descent; folding them into an
"evolution chain" would conflate structure with descent and violate the same-kind /
structural-naming rule (Spec §1, §3.4). The evolution chain therefore traverses the
two temporal-descent kinds **{succession, merger}** only; governance kinds remain in
M7's one-hop "Institutional relationships" section, where they already read
correctly. This is a faithful narrowing to the constitutional rule, fully reversible
if governance kinds are later wanted in the chain.

## Terminology

"Lineage" and "descent" are used as **structural** terms (Spec §1, §7.4 explicitly
name "a lineage of mentorship"). The reader-facing copy never says "school",
"tradition", "influence", "transmission", "inheritance", or "evolution" (the latter
is interpretive; the copy says "descent"/"succession", which are the canonical kind
terms). Copy is unit-tested to exclude that vocabulary.

## The read models

Two bounded recursive `SECURITY DEFINER` functions and **nothing else** — no table,
column, RLS change, or write path (M8 creates no Assertion). Each runs one recursive
CTE upstream and one downstream, depth-capped (16) and cycle-safe (a `path` array of
visited ids); each canonical edge is de-duplicated to its minimum depth (`distinct
on (rel_id)`) so a diamond never emits an edge twice; ordering is neutral (depth,
then endpoint name, then id) — never a rank, and chains are never ordered by length.
Each step carries `source` (the exact `organization_relationships` / `relationships`
row), `kind` (`{key,label,source_role}` from the kind vocabulary), `from`/`to`
`ProjectedNode`s in the assertion's own direction, `temporal`, `provenance`,
`direction`, and `depth`. Merged people are excluded from mentorship chains;
organizations have no `merged` state; a nonexistent (or merged focal person) → null.

## Feature module (reuse + harvest)

Reuses `parse-shared` primitives, `ProjectedNode`, the temporal/provenance kernels,
`RevealedPeriod`, and `ProvenanceAffordance`. Adds (harvested from **two** lineage
lenses in this slice): a shared `LineageStep` type + `parseStep` (in
`parse-lineage.ts`) + a shared `LineageStepList` component. `types` gains
`RelationshipKindRef`/`LineageStep`/the two documents; `read`/`derive`/`copy` gain
the two lenses; components add `OrganizationLineage`, `PersonMentorshipLineage`, and
`LineageStepList`. The directional sentence ("X is a documented {role} of Y") is
driven by the kind vocabulary's `source_role_label`, not hard-coded role words.

**Reader orientation.** Each lineage section anchors the **current subject** as a
non-interactive "you are reading" card placed **between** the antecedents/mentors
(above) and the successors/students (below), so a historian sees at a glance where
this institution/person sits in the documented descent. It is presentation only —
it names the focal node already in the document; it adds no data, capability, route,
or terminology.

## What it defers

Comparison C5 (enabled, not assigned); continuity & rupture (C3, M8.4); recurrence
(C4, M8.5); bounded pathway (C6, M8.6). Advising/interview/other directional kinds
are not folded into the mentorship chain (same-kind discipline); they could each
become their own homogeneous lens later. Exhibition inspection is deferred
(production-first; the three automated test layers exercise both lenses).
