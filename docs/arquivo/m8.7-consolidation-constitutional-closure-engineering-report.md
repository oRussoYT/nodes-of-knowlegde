# M8.7 — Revelation Engine: Consolidation and Constitutional Closure — Engineering Report

## 1. Executive verdict

M8.7 is the final milestone of M8. It adds **no new revelation capability** (no
C7, no comparison lens, no interpretation, no graph console). It treats M8.1–M8.6
as one Revelation Engine and reviews, consolidates, hardens, and formally closes
it. The milestone is an **audit-and-refine** pass, not a feature: the largest part
of the work is the review recorded here; the changes are deliberately few and each
produces a concrete improvement in correctness, integrity, or maintainability.

Six changes ship, in four categories:

- **One production correction (SQL).** `reveal_person_pathway` (C6) chose its
  minimal chain by a node-only path key, which is non-total: two distinct
  assertions between the same node pair produced an identical ordering key, so the
  chosen chain — and the exact canonical row each step decomposed to — was
  plan-dependent. This violated **determinism** and **decomposability** at once. A
  new append-only migration folds each edge's canonical source id into the path
  key, making the ordering total. Validated in a disposable Postgres 16.
- **One parser hardening (fail-closed).** Recurrence groups are now deduplicated
  by canonical source id before the ≥ 2 test, so a duplicated occurrence (e.g. an
  upstream JOIN fan-out) can never fabricate — or inflate the count of — a
  recurrence. This closed the one place a malformed revelation could have become a
  false historical statement rather than an honest absence.
- **Two consolidations that reduce integrity-relevant duplication.** The
  provenance envelope and the source-ref pointer — the two shapes that carry the
  engine's *evidence contract* — were parsed by copy-pasted blocks in six and five
  places. They are now single `parseProvenance` / `parseSourceRef` primitives in
  `parse-shared`, so the evidence contract can never drift between lenses. At the
  reading layer, the identical provenance footer (badge + affordance) and node
  doorway that every lens re-declared inline are now two shared components,
  `RevealedProvenanceFooter` and `NodeDoorway`.
- **One constitutional-closure test.** A single, total sweep asserts that the
  *whole* engine's reader-facing surface — every string in `revelationCopy` and
  the output of every deterministic `describe*` derivation — is free of the
  forbidden interpretive frames (similarity/metric/prestige tokens, and affirmative
  endpoint/ranking assertions). Previously each milestone shipped its own narrower
  check, so coverage was uneven.
- **One test-robustness fix.** The M8.1 cohort e2e used page-global locators where
  every other revelation spec scopes to its section; it now scopes to the cohorts
  section (while deliberately keeping the "a stranger appears nowhere" assertion
  page-global, which is strictly stronger).

**Validation status.** Everything validatable in this environment is green: the
pathway determinism fix was reproduced-then-fixed in a real Postgres 16 (the
choice is now invariant under physical row order and is the min-source-id chain);
the pure-logic engine (parse/derive/copy for M8.1→M8.6) passes **140/140** in the
tsx harness (up from 105, +35 for the closure sweep and the dedupe); project
**`tsc --noEmit` is clean** and **ESLint reports 0 errors** against the delivered
tree. The database (pgTAP), full build, and Playwright layers require the local
Mac (§13); the milestone is **certified complete only after the §13 Mac sequence
is green**.

## 2. The completed Revelation Engine

M8 **reveals**: it composes already-preserved (M1–M6), already-connected (M7)
Assertions into the deterministic patterns their joint truth entails, each
decomposable without remainder back to those Assertions. It infers, ranks, scores,
recommends, and interprets **nothing**. With M8.7 the engine implements every
revelation capability the M8 Specification named except the deliberately deferred
comparison (C5, §12):

| Capability | Operator(s) | Historical question answered |
|---|---|---|
| C1 co-presence (M8.1/M8.2) | `reveal_person_cohorts`, `reveal_organization_generations` | Who does the record document at the same institution as this person / at this institution, during overlapping years? |
| C2 lineage (M8.3) | `reveal_organization_lineage`, `reveal_person_mentorship_lineage` | What documented chain of succession/formation (institution) or mentorship (person) places this entity before and after others? |
| C3 continuity & rupture (M8.4) | `reveal_organization_continuity` | For each capacity documented here, what years does the record cover, where are its silences, and what is the institution's own recorded status? |
| C4 recurrence (M8.5) | `reveal_person_recurrence`, `reveal_organization_recurrence` | What phenomena does the record document more than once for this entity — the same role, kind of event, or kind of contribution — and how many times? |
| C6 bounded pathway (M8.6) | `reveal_person_pathway` | What documented chain of ≥ 2 explicit records links this person to a chosen entity through intermediaries — and exactly which steps compose it? |
| C5 comparison | *(deferred, never assigned)* | *(How do two documented lineages/pathways juxtapose? — an enabled affordance, not a milestone; §12.)* |

Each operator is a bounded `SECURITY DEFINER` read model returning a jsonb
*revelation document*; a fail-closed TypeScript parser turns that into a typed
reading projection or an honest absence; a pure derivation decides only *whether*
anything was revealed; deterministic copy states it; and a Server Component reads
it **inline** on the person or institution page — never a route, navigation entry,
dashboard, console, or diagram of its own.

## 3. The shared engine architecture

The six operators were built milestone by milestone but converged, by design, on
one architecture. M8.7's consolidations make that convergence explicit rather than
coincidental.

**Node projection.** Every projected entity is emitted in the canonical
`ProjectedNode` shape (`{type, id, label, secondary_label, href,
verification_status}`), parsed by the single `parseAnyNode`/`parseNode` in
`parse-shared`. The reading layer renders every such node through the new shared
`NodeDoorway`, so "every projected node is a doorway back to its record" is
expressed in one place.

**Evidence contract.** Every revealed element carries (a) the exact canonical row
it decomposes to — a `{type, id}` source ref — and (b) its provenance envelope —
`{sourceType, verificationStatus}`. After M8.7 both are parsed by single
primitives (`parseSourceRef`, `parseProvenance`) and disclosed by a single
`RevealedProvenanceFooter`. This is the engine's most important shared contract:
if it drifted between lenses, a revealed element could be shown without
decomposable evidence. It now cannot.

**Temporal honesty.** Every element carries its own period through the shared
Many-Clocks temporal kernel; no fabricated overlap/observation window is ever
synthesised.

**Fail-closed reads.** Every RPC is `SECURITY DEFINER`, pins `search_path`,
requires `auth.uid()` in-body, is revoked from `PUBLIC`, and is granted only to
`authenticated`; the internal `pathway_entity_node` helper is revoked from
`PUBLIC` with no client grant. Merged people are excluded at every projection and
endpoint; a merged/nonexistent focal returns null; an unresolvable target reads as
an honest "not found". Every parser degrades any malformed shape to an honest
absence rather than a partial or plausible-looking document.

**Determinism & neutral order.** Every set is composed by a fixed rule and ordered
by neutral, historical keys (names, years, depth, a neutral path key) with a
unique tiebreak — never a metric. M8.7 closes the one place this was not yet total
(the pathway path key).

**Reading composition.** All revelations read through the shared `ReadingSpine`,
after the canonical engines, in a coherent per-page order: person — cohorts →
mentorship lineage → recurrence → bounded pathway; institution — co-presence →
descent → continuity & rupture → recurrence. Each section states what it shows and
its honest limits; none behaves like analytics.

## 4. Constitutional audit (the closure)

Every operator was re-audited against the M8 §9.1 invariants; the findings are
recorded here because the audit *is* the milestone.

**Read-only.** No operator creates, updates, or grants write to any table; M8.7
adds only a `CREATE OR REPLACE` of one function. Confirmed across all six
migrations.

**Deterministic & reproducible.** All ordering keys are total after the pathway
fix. The recurrence, lineage, continuity, and co-presence operators were confirmed
to break every ordering tie on a unique key; the pathway operator did not, and now
does (§5). The dedupe fix removes the one input-shape (duplicate occurrences) that
could have made recurrence output depend on upstream fan-out.

**Explicit-evidence-only & decomposable.** Every revealed element carries its
canonical source row; the pathway chain-integrity guarantee (contiguous,
≥ 2 steps, count-agreeing) and the recurrence ≥ 2-distinct rule ensure nothing is
shown that a reader cannot walk back to records. The parser layer was audited for
partial-projection leaks and found clean; the previously-fixed `noUncheckedIndexedAccess`
guard in the pathway contiguity check is intact.

**Bounded where composition raises interpretive risk.** Lineage/mentorship recurse
to depth 16 with a path-array cycle guard; the pathway traversal is hop-capped at
4, node-`visited` cycle-guarded, and stops at the target — a small bound kept
constitutional (a longer chain is treated with *more* reserve), not a performance
knob.

**Fail-closed.** Confirmed uniformly (§3). No operator raises except the
intentional auth gate; every other error path returns null or an honest-absence
document.

**Honest about absence.** Every operator holds its absence states apart and never
collapses them: co-presence "no documented cohort yet"; continuity's four states
(continuation / rupture / evidentiary gap / unknown outcome); the pathway's
"no documented chain within four steps", never "not connected".

**Separate from interpretation.** Reader-facing copy was swept in full (§7). The
structural-naming rule holds throughout: a documented cohort is never a
"generation"; a pathway "connects" its ends but they are never "connected"; a
recurrence count is a count of records, never a measure of standing.

## 5. The production correction — pathway determinism (C6)

`reveal_person_pathway` returns the single minimal-length chain chosen by
`order by depth asc, path_key asc limit 1`. In M8.6 the `path_key` was built from
the traversed **node** sequence only. When two distinct assertions connect the
same node pair — a person with two documented participation periods at one
institution; two relationship kinds between the same people; a person credited on
one contribution in two capacities — the competing chains share an **identical**
`(depth, path_key)`, so `limit 1` broke the tie by physical/plan order. The
returned chain was always a valid documented chain, but *which* canonical row each
step decomposed to (its source/temporal/provenance) was not stable.

This was reproduced in a disposable Postgres 16 loaded with the pathway schema:
with two documented A→X participations, reversing the physical row order flipped
the chosen participation from one to the other. That is a determinism failure
(same inputs, different output across environments or after a VACUUM) and a
decomposability failure (the exact evidence a step cited was arbitrary).

**Fix (migration `20260817090000_close_revelation_engine.sql`).** A `CREATE OR
REPLACE` folds each edge's own canonical `source` id into the `path_key` at every
hop, making the ordering key **total**. Two parallel edges now sort by their
stable assertion id — a neutral identifier, not a metric, rank, or score — so the
single minimal chain is fully determined. Nothing else changes: reachability, the
≥ 2-step rule, the hop bound, cycle-safety, and every returned field are
identical. Post-fix the chosen chain is invariant under physical row order, is the
min-source-id chain, is equal across repeated calls, and (with the parallel edge
removed) returns exactly the prior 3-step chain. The migration is append-only; the
historical M8.6 migration is untouched. Two pgTAP assertions were added
(`revelation-pathway.test.sql`, plan 24 → 26) that pin the parallel-edge tie-break
to `min(id::text)` and confirm the parallel edge does not change the minimal chain
length; the underlying queries were verified against the sandbox with random
UUIDs.

## 6. The parser hardening — recurrence never fabricated

`parseGroup` enforced "recurrence is never once" on array length alone. If the RPC
emitted the same canonical row twice — a realistic upstream JOIN fan-out — two
identical occurrences passed the ≥ 2 test and the group reported `count: 2`, a
recurrence manufactured from a single record. The parser now deduplicates
occurrences by `source.type + source.id` before the cutoff and sets the count to
the distinct total, so a duplicated row can neither create nor inflate a
recurrence. Two unit tests lock the invariant (a duplicated role occurrence
collapses and the group is dropped; a group with one duplicate among three counts
2). This is the engine's fail-closed boundary doing exactly its job: a malformed
revelation degrades to an honest absence, never to a false claim.

## 7. The constitutional-closure sweep

`revelation-constitutional-closure.test.ts` gathers the entire reader-facing
corpus — `JSON.stringify(revelationCopy)` plus the output of every `describe*`
derivation across every category, status, direction, and target it can render —
and asserts the whole corpus is free of two lists. **Forbidden tokens** are
metric/similarity/prestige words (closeness, centrality, similarity, strength,
significance, influence, prestige, prominence, popularity, recommend, embedding,
probability) that carry an interpretive frame in any grammatical form and have no
honest reason to appear. **Forbidden assertions** are the affirmative
endpoint/ranking claims the engine refuses ("is connected to", "most influential",
"ranked by", …). The distinction is deliberate and load-bearing: the copy
*legitimately negates* some of these ideas — "not a connection between its ends",
"never ranked", "never ordered or scored by length" — so a bare ban on
"connection"/"ranked"/"scored" would reject honest copy; only the affirmative
construction is banned, and only never-appearing words are banned outright. The
sweep also includes a corpus-anchor guard (nine phrases from across M8.1→M8.6) so
a future refactor cannot make it vacuously pass, and an explicit endpoint-rule
check (the pathway summary "connects" its ends, never "is connected"). This
replaces the six divergent per-operator lists with one total, single-sourced
guarantee.

## 8. Consolidations at the parser and reading layers

**`parseProvenance` / `parseSourceRef`.** The provenance envelope was hand-parsed
in six places and the source ref in five, always identically. Because these carry
the evidence contract, drift here would be a silent integrity bug, so this is a
correctness-adjacent consolidation, not a cosmetic one. Both are now single
primitives in `parse-shared`, called by `parseAnchor`, `parseMember`,
`parseOccurrence`, and the lineage/continuity/pathway step parsers. Behaviour is
unchanged (the pure-logic suite passes unchanged); the contract now lives in one
place.

**`RevealedProvenanceFooter` / `NodeDoorway`.** Five leaf components
(`CohortMember`, `LineageStepList`, `PathwayStepList`, `PracticeCoverage`,
`RecurrenceGroupList`) each re-declared the identical provenance footer (the
verification badge + the `ProvenanceAffordance`) and a local doorway helper
(`Doorway`/`StepEndpoint`/`PersonDoorway`/`OccurrenceTitle`). Both are now shared
components with byte-identical output, so the decomposability affordance and the
doorway contract are each defined once. Output identity was preserved exactly
(same elements, class names, and props), so the accessibility/reading behaviour is
unchanged; `tsc` and ESLint confirm the refactor.

## 9. Rejected changes (reviewed and deliberately not made)

The review surfaced several further candidates; each was rejected for a concrete
reason, per the milestone's "improve where it helps, do not churn" mandate.

- **A shared SQL node-projection helper across all six migrations.** The
  `ProjectedNode` jsonb is built inline ~26 times. Generalising the M8.6
  `pathway_entity_node` helper across all operators would be a large `CREATE OR
  REPLACE` of five historical functions for **zero** behavioural change, and most
  inline projections reuse a row the query already joined (for ordering) whereas a
  helper re-selects by id — trading a hot-path join for per-node lookups. Rejected
  as cosmetic-with-a-perf-cost; the per-migration versions are identical in shape
  and individually correct.
- **Adding a `person` ProjectedNode to `reveal_person_cohorts`.** M8.1 returns
  `person_id` but not a `person` node, unlike the later person-focal operators — a
  real contract asymmetry. But nothing reads it (the M8.1 parser and surface use
  `person_id`), so adding it would churn the oldest operator's contract for a
  speculative future consumer. Rejected under "no speculative infrastructure";
  recorded here so the asymmetry is known.
- **A `RevelationSection` wrapper across the eight top-level components.** The
  section shell (landmark + h2 + "what this shows" + empty state + limits) is the
  largest structural duplication, but the eight sections differ meaningfully (anchor
  sub-sections, a status heading, per-lens empty states, the pathway's four
  states), so a single wrapper would need enough props to lose the per-lens clarity
  that makes each section readable — and its only certifying test is the Mac
  e2e/axe suite. The mechanical duplication (footer + doorway) was extracted
  instead; the section shell was left per-lens by design.
- **Re-ordering the institution page so revelations follow `significance`/`legacy`.**
  The revelations currently render after contributions and before the reserved
  records/significance/legacy narrative. Moving them is a page-structure change
  whose only validator is the Mac e2e suite, and the current order was chosen and
  shipped across M8.2–M8.5. Left as-is; noted for a future reading-experience pass.
- **Dropping revelation section headings from `text-xl` to `text-sm`.** The
  revelation h2s are visually heavier than the institution page's narrative-facet
  h2s. But they are consistent across all four revelation sections and with the
  other engine sections; this is an aesthetic judgement with no correctness content
  and no in-environment validator. Left as-is.
- **Re-writing the e2e page-quality boilerplate into a shared helper.** The six
  specs share a ~30-line a11y/375px/dark/console block. A shared helper is a
  genuine maintainability win but touches all six un-runnable-here specs; deferred
  as a test-only refactor to avoid shipping an un-certifiable change in a closure
  milestone.

## 10. Limitations (unchanged and accepted)

The engine's standing limitations are unchanged: undated participations are
excluded from co-presence and continuity (an honest limit, stated on each
surface); one representative overlapping participation is shown per co-present
member; the pathway shows one minimal chain (others may exist; stated in its
limits) and is hop-capped at 4 (a longer chain reads as an honest absence, not a
silent cut); event nodes carry a title but no page; recurrence is single-entity
repetition (the dyadic co-appearance count of Spec §3.5 is deferred). On very
dense production hubs the pathway traversal cost should be observed via query plans
on the Mac and the cap lowered if needed (a Blueprint composition-cost note, not a
correctness issue).

## 11. The M8 / M9 boundary

M8 reveals *that* the record composes a pattern and *how* — a cohort, a descent, a
coverage, a recurrence count, a chain of N steps — and hands M9 a clean,
decomposable substrate: ordered lists of canonical assertion ids with their
provenance, asserting nothing about what any pattern *means*. Whether a documented
co-presence was a collaboration, whether a lineage transmitted anything, whether a
chain signifies a connection — every such judgement is **M9's interpretation**, or
the reader's, never the engine's. M8.7 sharpens this boundary by making the copy
sweep total: the surface now provably never crosses from revealing into
interpreting.

## 12. Deferred: comparison (C5)

Comparison (C5) — the juxtaposition of two documented lineages or pathways — is the
only remaining C-capability and remains a **deferred, enabled affordance, never
assigned to a milestone**. It was enabled by the M8.3 lineage juxtaposition and is
explicitly "not a standalone page"; it is not built, designed, or scaffolded here.
M8 is otherwise complete (C1–C4, C6).

## 13. Ratified next architectural work (recorded, not built)

Two post-M8 architectural milestones are **ratified as the next work before M9**
and are recorded here only so the closure is honest about what follows; **neither
is designed or implemented in M8.7**:

1. **Projects as First-Class Historical Actors** — promoting a research project
   from an implicit grouping to a canonical entity that can participate, connect,
   and be revealed like a person or institution. The Revelation Engine's
   node-neutral `ProjectedNode` contract and heterogeneous traversal are already
   shaped to admit a fifth entity type without a new lens; nothing is committed to
   that shape here.
2. **Historical Operators Architecture** — a general framework in which revelation
   operators (and, later, interpretation operators) are declared, composed, and
   tested uniformly rather than authored one migration at a time. M8.7's
   consolidations (single evidence-contract parsers, shared projection/footer/doorway,
   the single constitutional sweep) are the first evidence that such a framework is
   worth extracting; the framework itself is future work.

## 14. Compatibility notes

**First-class Projects.** The engine reads over canonical tables and projects the
node-neutral `ProjectedNode`; the pathway traversal already composes across four
entity types. Admitting a fifth (Project) is additive at the SQL layer and free at
the parser/reading layer (`parseAnyNode` and `NodeDoorway` are type-driven). No M8
decision blocks it.

**Historical Operators Architecture.** The M8.7 shared primitives
(`parseProvenance`, `parseSourceRef`, `parseAnyNode`, `NodeDoorway`,
`RevealedProvenanceFooter`, the constitutional sweep) are exactly the reusable
contract surface such an architecture would formalise. They are shipped as shared
functions/components today; promoting them to a declared operator framework is a
clean next step, not a rewrite.
