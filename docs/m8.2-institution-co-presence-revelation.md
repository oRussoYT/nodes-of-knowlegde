# M8.2 — Revelation Engine: institution-surface co-presence (documented co-presence)

Status: implemented (pending local Mac validation). See
`docs/decisions/0018-revelation-engine.md` (amended for M8.2), the M8 Definitive
Specification, and the M8 Engineering Blueprint.

## What this is

M8.1 revealed, from one **person's** vantage, the documented cohorts that person
belonged to. **M8.2 reveals the same co-presence relation from the
institution's vantage**: for a focal institution, which of its documented
participants the record places there **at the same time** as which others. It is
the institution mirror of `reveal_person_cohorts` — the intersection lens (M8
Specification §7.1, the Camp 41 cohort): the restatement of several participation
Assertions, arranged so their coincidence in place and time becomes visible.

It reads **inline** on `/institutions/[organizationId]`, after the institution's
Participation and Contributions engines. It is not a destination and adds no
navigation, no route, no metric, and no visualization.

## Scope confirmation (from the M8 Engineering Blueprint)

The blueprint's M8.2 — "Co-presence complete (C1, C5): institution generations and
event company; the comparison affordance where two records are jointly in view" —
names three parts. Only **institution generations** has a ratified production
surface today, so this slice implements that part and defers the other two on a
strictly architectural basis (recorded here and in ADR-0018):

- **Event company (C1 on events)** is deferred: events are not a canonical reading
  destination (no `/events/*` route; `ProjectedNode.href` is null for events), and
  M8 creates no new destination. It awaits an event reading surface.
- **Comparison (C5)** is deferred to M8.3: the blueprint states C5 is "not a
  standalone page… offered where two records are already jointly in view," and its
  named host — the institution lineage-counterpart juxtaposition — is C2/lineage
  (M8.3). C5 will ride that host when it exists.

This deferral was surfaced as a scope contradiction and approved before
implementation; it is not a silent narrowing.

## Terminology

The blueprint's shorthand is "institution generations," but the M8 Specification
(§2) explicitly flags calling a documented cohort **"a generation"** as
*interpretation* (a claim about shared identity the assertions do not make).
Per the structural-naming rule, the internal capability keeps the blueprint's name
(`reveal_organization_generations`), while the **reader-facing surface says
"documented co-presence," never "generation."**

## The read model

`reveal_organization_generations(p_organization_id uuid) → jsonb`, one bounded
`SECURITY DEFINER` function and nothing else — no table, no column, no RLS change,
no write path (M8 creates no Assertion).

For an authenticated reader and a focal institution: among the **dated**,
**non-merged** participations at that institution, it composes, for each
participant who has at least one temporally-overlapping co-participant, an
**anchor** carrying (a) that participant as a `ProjectedNode` doorway, (b) **all**
of their own documented participations there (so both sides of every overlap are
decomposable), and (c) the **co-present** people — others whose documented period
there overlaps theirs — each carrying their `participations` `source` id, temporal
payload, provenance, and capacity.

Overlap is the standard interval test on stored dates
(`a.start ≤ coalesce(b.end,∞) AND b.start ≤ coalesce(a.end,∞)`), **pairwise** — the
exact M8.1 rule. Anchors are ordered by participant `display_name`, co-present
people by `display_name`; a person with several overlapping stints is represented
once (earliest overlapping), linking onward. The focal institution and every node
are projected in the canonical `ProjectedNode` shape
(`type`/`id`/`label`/`secondary_label`/`href`/`verification_status`).

## The boundary that keeps this revelation and not inference

Both a **shared institution** and a **temporal overlap** are required. A shared
institution alone, a non-overlapping period, or an undated participation is **not**
co-presence. It is shown as a documented co-presence — a record that people were
here in the same years — and is **never** promoted to a relationship,
collaboration, community, school, or influence. Every co-present person decomposes
to its exact `participations` row; the pattern adds nothing to the joint content
of those assertions. No inference, similarity, ranking, centrality, recommendation,
clustering (no overlap "windows"/generational grouping), probabilistic or causal
claim, or interpretation appears anywhere.

## Feature module (reuse)

`src/features/revelation/` gains: `parse-shared.ts` (the shared fail-closed node/
capacity/temporal/anchor/member parse primitives, **harvested** from M8.1's
`parse.ts` now that a second co-presence lens demonstrates the identical need — the
M6.3 kernel-extraction precedent; M8.1's `parse.ts` now imports them, its public
behaviour unchanged); `parse-organization.ts`; `types.ts` gains
`OrganizationGenerationsDocument`/`GenerationAnchor` (reusing `CohortAnchor`/
`CohortMember`); `read.ts` gains `getOrganizationGenerations`; `derive.ts` gains
`buildOrganizationGenerationsView`; `copy.ts` gains neutral `organizationGenerations`
copy; components add `OrganizationGenerations` and `OrganizationGenerationAnchor`,
**reusing** `CohortMember` and `RevealedPeriod` and the shared temporal/provenance
kernels and the M7 `ProjectedNode`.

## What it defers

Event company (no event surface); comparison C5 (M8.3 host); and every later lens —
lineage (C2, M8.3), continuity & rupture (C3, M8.4), recurrence (C4, M8.5), bounded
pathway (C6, M8.6). Exhibition inspection of this lens is deferred (production-first;
the three automated test layers exercise it deterministically).
