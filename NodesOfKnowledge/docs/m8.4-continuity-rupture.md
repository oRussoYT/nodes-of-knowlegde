# M8.4 — Revelation Engine: continuity & rupture (C3)

Status: implemented (pending local Mac validation). See
`docs/decisions/0018-revelation-engine.md` (amended for M8.4), the M8 Definitive
Specification (§3.4 sequence composition, §3.9), and the M8 Engineering Blueprint.

## What this is

M8.1/M8.2 revealed **co-presence**; M8.3 revealed **lineage** (a chain of
same-kind directional Assertions). M8.4 reveals a third pattern family:
**continuity & rupture** — whether a documented practice at one institution is
**sustained** or **interrupted** across a long span. One lens, read **inline** on
the institution page:

- **`reveal_organization_continuity(uuid)`** — for one institution, the documented
  **coverage** of each participation capacity over time (the merged year-intervals
  of the dated participation records in that capacity, and the silences between
  them), alongside the institution's own explicit **status** and **closure**.

It composes a time-ordered series of already-preserved, already-dated
participation Assertions per capacity into their entailed coverage, decomposable
without remainder back to each participation.

## The central discipline: four states, never collapsed

This milestone exists to hold four states apart. The governing rule: *the system
may reveal what the record already demonstrates; it may not propose what the
record merely makes plausible.* Concretely:

- **Documented continuation** requires **explicit continuation evidence**: a
  capacity whose **latest** documented interval is **open-ended** (a participation
  with no end date / `is_ongoing`). The record itself says the belonging is still
  current. This is the *only* continuation signal — chronological adjacency to
  "now" is never one.
- **Documented rupture** requires **explicit termination evidence**: the
  institution carrying a terminal status in the explicit vocabulary
  (`closed` / `absorbed` / `succeeded` / `merged`), with its closure date when
  recorded. This is an institution-level assertion (`organizations.status`), and
  it is **never propagated** to claim that a particular practice ceased on the
  closure date.
- **Evidentiary gap**: a silence **between** two documented intervals in a
  capacity. It is reported as a break in the record and **never** as a
  demonstrated end (Spec §3.9: "must not present a gap as a demonstrated end").
- **Unknown outcome**: a capacity whose latest documented interval is **closed**,
  with no terminal status to speak to it. The honest statement is *the available
  record does not document what followed* — **not** *the activity ended*.

The read model returns only the raw, decomposable structure (coverage spans, the
silences, the explicit status/closure). It computes **no** continuity/rupture
verdict and stores none; the four states are read deterministically from that
structure at the surface and are kept apart in the copy.

## Schema grounding (reported, not silently resolved)

The crux "gap vs. rupture" is resolved by grounding **rupture** in the explicit
`organizations.status` vocabulary added by the Institution Engine
(`active | historical | closed | dormant | merged | absorbed | succeeded |
provisional | status_unknown`) and its `closure_date`. Only the four terminal
keys read as a documented rupture (category `ended`); `dormant` reads as `paused`,
`historical` as `historical`, `provisional`/`status_unknown` as `unknown`, and
`active` as continuation of the institution. A **gap** is never derived from
status — it is only ever a silence between two dated participation intervals.
This keeps "the record merely stops" (unknown outcome) and "the record documents
an end" (rupture) grounded in *different* explicit assertions, never conflated.

## The read model

One bounded `SECURITY DEFINER` function and **nothing else** — no table, column,
RLS change, or write path (M8 creates no Assertion). Per capacity, over the
**dated** participation records only (undated participations cannot speak to
coverage; merged people are excluded), it runs a gaps-and-islands interval merge
**at year granularity** (the overview is by year): overlapping *and adjacent*
years merge into one coverage span; a start year two or more years past the
running maximum end year opens a new span, and the fully uncovered years between
are an evidentiary gap. An open-ended record has an effective end year of
`+infinity` (a large sentinel), so it absorbs everything after it into one open
span — which is why an open span is always the **last** in its capacity, and why
continuation is only ever read from the latest span. Coverage is thus
**conservative about gaps**: only a whole-year silence is ever shown as one. Each
coverage span carries the exact participation rows it merges (each its canonical
`participations` id + person `ProjectedNode` + full stored temporal + provenance),
so a span decomposes without remainder. Ordering is neutral (capacity label, then
start year); there is no metric, no ranking, no "longest run". A nonexistent
institution → null.

## Feature module (reuse)

Reuses `parse-shared` primitives (`parseNode`, `parseTemporal`, `parseCapacity`,
`asSourceType`, `asVerification`, `asPrecision`), `ProjectedNode`, the
temporal/provenance kernels, `RevealedPeriod`, and `ProvenanceAffordance`. Adds
`parse-continuity.ts` (fail-closed), types
(`OrganizationContinuityDocument`, `Practice`, `CoverageSpan`, `CoverageGap`,
`DocumentedStatus`, `ClosureMoment`), `read.getOrganizationContinuity`,
`derive.buildOrganizationContinuityView` (reads the four states), copy
(`organizationContinuity` + `describeCoverageSpan`/`describeCoverageGap`/
`describeContinuityOutcome`/`describeDocumentedStatus`), and two components
(`OrganizationContinuity`, `PracticeCoverage`). No new shared primitive was
harvested (one lens does not demonstrate repetition).

## Terminology

Structural terms only: *documented continuation / interruption / gap / coverage /
recorded status / closure*. The reader-facing copy never says *tradition
persisted*, *intellectual continuity*, *institutional resilience*, *knowledge
survived*, *legacy*, or *influence*; it never calls a gap an ending, and never
dates a practice's end from the institution's closure. Copy is unit-tested to
exclude that vocabulary.

## Integration

Inline on `/institutions/[organizationId]`, inside the shared `ReadingSpine`,
after the M8.3 institutional descent. No new route, navigation, dashboard, metric,
or graph. The section shows the institution's recorded status apart from the
per-capacity coverage, each capacity's spans and gaps, and one honest outcome
sentence per capacity.

## What it defers

Recurrence (C4, M8.5); bounded pathway (C6, M8.6); comparison (C5, enabled
earlier, not assigned). A person-surface continuity lens (the continuity of one
person's practices) is a possible future homogeneous lens, not built here.
