# M8.1 — Revelation Engine: co-presence (documented cohorts)

Status: implemented (pending local Mac validation). See
`docs/decisions/0018-revelation-engine.md` for the ratified architecture, the M8
Definitive Specification for the law, and the M8 Engineering Blueprint for the
milestone decomposition.

## Purpose

M1–M6 **preserved** the record; M7 **connected** it (one hop); M8 **reveals** it.
M8.1 delivers the Revelation kernel and its first lens end-to-end:
**co-presence** on the person. It composes several `participations` Assertions
into the **documented cohorts** a person belonged to — the other people
documented at the **same institution** during an **overlapping** period — each
decomposable back to the participation record that establishes it. It is the
intersection lens (the prosopographer's instrument): the joint restatement of
explicit participation Assertions, arranged so their coincidence in place and
time becomes visible. It reveals what the record already demonstrates; it infers,
ranks, recommends, and interprets nothing.

## Architecture (ADR-0018)

A **derived, read-only** read model over the canonical record. One additive
migration adds exactly one bounded `SECURITY DEFINER` function and **no table,
no column, no RLS change, no write path** — M8 creates no Assertion.

### The read model — `reveal_person_cohorts(uuid)`

For an authenticated reader and a focal person: for each institution the person
is **datedly** part of, the **other** (non-merged) people whose participation
there **overlaps** the person's, each carrying its canonical `participations`
source row, temporal payload, and provenance; plus the focal person's own
anchoring participation(s) at that institution. Overlap is the interval test on
the stored dates with an unrecorded/open end treated as open
(`coalesce(end_date, 'infinity')`). Undated participations cannot establish
temporal co-presence and are excluded. Cohorts are ordered by institution name,
members by display_name — neutral and historical, never a metric. A
merged/nonexistent focal returns null; merged members are omitted. `SECURITY
DEFINER`, `search_path` pinned, `auth.uid()` enforced, revoked from `PUBLIC`,
granted to `authenticated`; tables stay deny-by-default.

### The reading shape (`src/features/revelation/`)

`types.ts` (the projection: `PersonCohortsDocument` → `Cohort` → `CohortMember`
/ `CohortAnchor`, reusing the M7 `ProjectedNode` and the shared temporal/
provenance kernels); `read.ts` (wraps the RPC, fails closed to null); `parse.ts`
(defensive, drops any malformed member/cohort, never invents one); `derive.ts`
(pure — empty check + preserves the read model's order, no re-ranking);
`copy.ts` (deterministic sentences and honest copy; no AI, no metric/ranking/
similarity/recommendation/social language — unit-tested).

### The reading experience

The cohort reads **inline** on `/people/[personId]`, after the person's own
engines, through the shared `ReadingSpine`: a "Documented cohorts" section that
states what it shows, then either the cohorts (each a `CohortGroup`: the
institution as a doorway, the focal anchor, and the members as `CohortMember`
rows with a deterministic sentence, their own period, a verification badge for
provisional/disputed, and provenance one keyboard-operable gesture away via the
shared `ProvenanceAffordance`) or a dignified honest empty state; and, when a
cohort is revealed, the honest **Limits of this view** note ("the documented
cohort, not the true one"). No new route, no navigation entry, no visualization —
a vantage that opens within the reading. Textual and fully usable without any
diagram.

## Co-presence is revelation, not inference

Both a shared institution **and** a temporal overlap are required. A shared
institution alone, a non-overlapping period, or an undated participation is
**not** co-presence. Co-presence is shown as a documented co-presence — a record
that people were at the same institution in the same years — and is **never**
promoted to a relationship, collaboration, similarity, or influence. Whether the
co-presence meant anything is the reader's question and M9's interpretation.

## Copy

All member sentences are derived deterministically from the participation
Assertion (the capacity label). No AI prose. No metric, ranking, recommendation,
prestige, popularity, influence, similarity, or social/engagement language. The
section states plainly that it shows the documented cohort, not the true one,
and that absence is never proof no co-presence exists.

## Testing

- **pgTAP** (`supabase/tests/database/revelation.test.sql`, 27 tests):
  existence; authorization + deny-by-default; the overlap boundary
  (shared-institution-alone, non-overlapping, and undated are NOT members;
  merged omitted; different institution never a cohort); canonical-source
  preservation (every member decomposes to its `participations` row); temporal
  and capacity preservation; neutral ordering; the focal anchor; the no-inference
  guarantee (every member justified only by a participation); honest empty/
  merged/nonexistent behaviour; and determinism.
- **Vitest** (`tests/unit/revelation-{parse,derive,copy}.test.ts`): fail-closed
  parsing (drops malformed members/cohorts, rejects mistyped nodes, never
  invents), the empty-absence and order-preservation of the view, and the
  deterministic copy — unit-tested for the absence of metric/ranking/similarity/
  recommendation/social language and PDBFF-specific vocabulary (Node
  Independence).
- **Playwright** (`tests/e2e/revelation.spec.ts`): unauthenticated redirect; a
  revealed cohort reading inline with the institution doorway, the member link,
  the deterministic sentence, provenance, and the honest limits; a person at a
  different institution never revealed (honest absence); a sole participant's
  dignified empty state; keyboard operability; axe (no serious/critical); one
  h1; 375px; light/dark; console/hydration cleanliness.
