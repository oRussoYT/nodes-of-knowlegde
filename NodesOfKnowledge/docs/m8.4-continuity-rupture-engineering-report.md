## 2. Exact scope implemented

C3 continuity & rupture, one lens: the documented per-capacity **coverage** of one
institution over time (merged year-intervals + the silences between them),
alongside its own explicit terminal **status** and **closure**, inline on
`/institutions/[organizationId]`. The operator design ratified by the user is
**"per-capacity coverage + terminal status."**

## 3. Historical question answered

"For each capacity people are documented in at this institution, what years does
the record cover, where does it fall silent, and what does the institution's own
record say about whether it continued or ended?"

## 4. Deterministic rule

Per capacity, over the **dated** participation records (undated excluded; merged
people excluded), a gaps-and-islands interval merge at **year** granularity:
overlapping and adjacent years merge into one coverage span; a start year ≥ 2
years past the running maximum end year opens a new span, and the fully uncovered
years between are an **evidentiary gap**. An open-ended record (effective end year
`+infinity`) absorbs everything after it into one open span — so an open span is
always the last, and continuation is read only from the latest span. The
institution's own status maps to a neutral category (`ended` only for
closed/absorbed/succeeded/merged). Ordering is neutral (capacity label, then start
year); no metric, no ranking.

## 5. Canonical source assertions

`participations` (dated, non-merged person) + `participation_capacities` +
`people` + `organizations` (`status`, `closure_date`, `closure_precision`). No new
table, no write.

## 7. Database / security audit

One additive function; no table/column/RLS/write. `SECURITY DEFINER`,
`search_path` pinned to `public, pg_temp`, `auth.uid()` required in-body, revoked
from `PUBLIC`, granted to `authenticated`. Undated participations excluded (an
unknown period cannot speak to coverage); merged people excluded; nonexistent
institution → null. Bounded and deterministic — a finite window computation over
the explicit records (Spec §6.6). Every referenced column was verified against the
device migrations during scope determination: `participations`
(`capacity`, the temporal columns, `date_is_unknown`, `verification_status`),
`participation_capacities` (`key`, `label`), `organizations`
(`status`, `closure_date`, `closure_precision`, `verification_status`).

## 8. Projection / parser contract audit

Every projected node is emitted in the canonical `ProjectedNode` shape (`type`,
`id`, `label`, `secondary_label`, `href`, `verification_status`); pgTAP locks it
(focal org node and a coverage participation's person node assert `type`/`label`).
The parser (`parse-continuity.ts`) reuses the shared `parseNode`/`parseTemporal`/
`parseCapacity` primitives, is fail-closed (drops a span whose open flag disagrees
with its end year, a participation with a missing/mistyped person or missing
source, a malformed gap; drops a practice with no surviving span; degrades an
unrecognizable status category to `unknown` and a malformed closure to null
without failing the document), and Vitest confirms the SQL→parser shape and the
dropping discipline.

## 9. Temporal / provenance audit

Each participation shows its own stored dates (shared temporal kernel) and its
full provenance envelope, and decomposes to its canonical `participations` row.
The coverage extent is summarised by year for the overview; nothing is inferred or
back-filled. A gap is shown as a silence in the record, never a filled or ended
interval. The institution's closure date is shown as its own explicit moment,
never used to date the end of any capacity.

## 10. The four honest states (the milestone's core)

Held apart in structure, derivation, and copy, and each pinned by a test:

- **Continuation** — a practice whose latest span is open-ended
  (`latestIsOpen`); copy: "documents this capacity as still current." The only
  continuation signal.
- **Rupture** — the institution's terminal status (category `ended`), with its
  closure date; copy: "documents this institution as closed/absorbed/succeeded/
  merged [in YEAR]." Institution-level; never propagated to a practice.
- **Evidentiary gap** — a whole-year silence between two spans; copy: "a gap in
  the record, not a documented ending."
- **Unknown outcome** — a practice whose latest span is closed; copy: "the record
  does not document what followed." Never "the activity ended."

pgTAP proves the non-collapse directly: a closed institution (rupture, closure
1998) whose researcher coverage ends 1990 reports the practice's span ending at
**1990**, never back-filled to 1998.

## 13. Defects found and fixed

One, caught in self-review before delivery: an initial date-granularity
interval-merge would have flagged **within-year and adjacent-year silences as
gaps** (e.g. a record ending 1975 and one starting 1976 shown as a gap). Since the
overview is by year, this over-claimed interruption. Fixed by merging at **year**
granularity with an adjacency rule (`start_year > prev_max_end_year + 1` opens a
new span), so only a whole-year silence is ever a gap — the conservative,
honest choice for a year-level summary. No production logic defect otherwise.

## 14. Constitutional audit (M8 §9.1)

Read-only (one function); no inference (coverage is the union of explicit dated
intervals; continuation only from an explicit open-ended record; rupture only from
the explicit status vocabulary; a gap never derived from status or from a missing
record); no metric/ranking/centrality (neutral order; no "longest run");
deterministic + bounded (finite window computation; pgTAP determinism);
decomposable (every span → its canonical participation rows; person a doorway);
order not causation (coverage over time, never "sustained because"); temporally
honest (own dates, year-summary, no fill); fail-closed security; honest
incompleteness (limits + empty state; a gap distinguished from an end, a
record-stop from an ending); no new destination (inline; nav unchanged); no
interpretation (structural continuation/interruption/gap, never tradition/
resilience/legacy/influence/persistence — copy unit-tested to exclude them).

## 15. Limitations

Coverage summarised by year (sub-year precision remains in each participation's
own period, decomposable, but the span/gap overview is year-level — noted, not
silent). A gap is only a whole-year silence (conservative). The lens is
institution-scoped; a person-surface continuity lens is deferred. Undated
participations are excluded by construction (they cannot speak to coverage).
